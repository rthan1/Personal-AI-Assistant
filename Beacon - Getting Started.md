# Getting Started with Beacon

Beacon is a personal AI assistant you text over iMessage. It reads your Google Calendar, reminds you before events, tells you when to leave, and more. There's no app to download.

## What you need

- An iPhone with iMessage turned on
- A Google account with Google Calendar
- About 5 minutes

---

## Step 1: Ask to be a tester

Beacon is in testing, so Google only lets approved testers connect their calendar. Before you sign up, text Ethan at **(717) 894-8470** and ask to be added as a tester.

Include:

- Your name
- The **Google (Gmail) address** you use for Google Calendar
- The **iPhone number** you'll text Beacon from

Example text:

> Hi Ethan, can you add me as a Beacon tester? I'm Alex Smith, my Google account is alex.smith@gmail.com.

Wait until Ethan confirms you've been added. If you go ahead before that, Google will block the calendar connection in Step 6.

---

## Step 2: Open the sign-up page

On your iPhone, go to:

**https://detached-tasty-pronto.ngrok-free.dev**

The first time, you may see an ngrok page saying you're about to visit a site. Tap **Visit Site**. This only appears once.

## Step 3: Enter your phone number

On the **"Your calendar, over iMessage"** page:

1. Type your iPhone number.
2. Tap **Get started**.

## Step 4: Text the assistant

The **"You're on the list"** page shows a number to text.

1. Tap the **Text** button, or open Messages and text that number anything, like "hi".
2. Save the number as a contact (for example "Beacon"). This is the number your assistant always texts from.

Within a few seconds it replies with your **personal sign-up link**.

> **Nothing came back?** Make sure iMessage sends from your phone number, not just your email: go to **Settings > Messages > Send & Receive** and check your phone number under "Start New Conversations From".

## Step 5: Fill out the sign-up form

Tap the link in the reply. It works for **1 hour** and is tied to your phone number, so don't share it. If it expires, text the assistant again for a new one.

On the **"Set up your assistant"** form:

| Field | What to enter |
|---|---|
| Name | What the assistant should call you |
| Timezone | Filled in automatically; change it only if it's wrong |
| Home address *(optional)* | Used for "when should I leave?" travel times |
| How do you usually get around? | Drive, transit, walk, or bike |
| Daily briefing *(optional)* | A time to get a text each day with that day's events. Leave it blank for no briefing. |

Tap **Continue to Google Calendar**.

## Step 6: Connect Google Calendar

1. Pick the **same Google account** you gave Ethan in Step 1.
2. You'll see **"Google hasn't verified this app"**. That's expected while Beacon is in testing. Tap **Continue**.
3. Allow access to **see and edit events on your calendar**, then tap **Continue**.

Beacon can see and edit your primary calendar, but it **always asks you first** before adding, changing, or deleting anything.

> **Getting "Access blocked" or "app is in testing" instead?** You haven't been added as a tester yet, or you picked a different Google account. Text Ethan to check (Step 1).

## Step 7: You're all set

The **"You're all set"** page means your calendar is connected. Go back to Messages and try:

> what's on my calendar today?

## Things to try

| Text something like... | What happens |
|---|---|
| "What's on tomorrow?" | Lists your events |
| "When should I leave for my 3pm?" | Travel time with live traffic from your home address |
| "Find coffee near my 2pm meeting" | Nearby places |
| "Add lunch with Sam Friday at noon" | Proposes the event; reply **yes** to save it |
| "Move my dentist appointment to 4" | Proposes the change and warns about conflicts; reply **yes** to save it |
| "Invite sam@example.com to lunch Friday" | Proposes the invite; Google emails them after you reply **yes** |
| "Remind me to call Mom at 6" | Sends you a text at 6 |
| "Remind me 15 minutes before every event" | Turns on automatic event reminders |
| "Change my briefing to 7:30am" or "Stop my daily briefing" | Updates your daily briefing |
| "Remember I'm vegetarian" | Saves a note it uses later; ask it to forget anytime |
| "help" | Lists what it can do |

**Calendar changes always take two texts.** The assistant shows exactly what it will do, and nothing is saved until you reply **yes** in a separate message.

## Troubleshooting

- **The sign-up link expired.** Text the assistant anything and it sends a new one.
- **"Calendar not connected" or "Couldn't connect your calendar".** Text the assistant for a new link and try Step 6 again. Make sure you tap **Continue** and allow calendar access.
- **It says your calendar connection expired.** While Beacon is in testing, Google ends the connection about every **7 days**. The assistant texts you a reconnect link; tap it and repeat Step 6.
- **Still stuck?** Text Ethan at **(717) 894-8470**.
